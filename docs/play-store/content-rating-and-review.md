# Content rating, target audience and reviewer notes

## Content rating questionnaire (IARC)

Category: Utility, productivity, communication or other. Answer **No** to every content
question (violence, sexual content, language, controlled substances, gambling, user
interaction/sharing, location sharing, digital purchases). Expected rating: Everyone / PEGI 3.

## Target audience

18 and over (primary audience: working adults). Not designed for children.

## Ads

No ads.

## App access instructions for reviewers

UnderTone has no login. Full functionality needs the permissions granted during the setup
tour. Suggested reviewer text:

> No account is needed. On first launch, follow the setup tour and grant Notification access,
> Do Not Disturb access and the call-screening role when asked. To see the core feature: on
> the Apps screen set any app to "Hidden" outside hours, send yourself a notification from
> that app, and open the Held inbox on the Home screen. UnderTone has no internet permission;
> all processing stays on the device.

## Closed testing (new personal developer account)

Production access needs a closed test with at least 12 testers opted in for 14 consecutive
days. Plan: 14 testers for a 2-week closed test; share the opt-in link; remind testers not to
leave the test before day 14.
