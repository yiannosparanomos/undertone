## Short description (≤80)

Decide what rings, what stays quiet and what waits. No internet, ever.

## Full description

UnderTone gives you calm control over notifications and calls, without sending a single byte
off your phone.

**Choose what reaches you**
• Four levels per app: Ring, Silent, Hidden until later, Blocked
• Active hours per app: work apps ring on weekdays and wait quietly on weekends
• Set every app at once, then fine-tune the ones you care about

**Calls, your way**
• Ring tiers: Everyone, Contacts, Allow list or Nobody
• Repeat callers: someone on your allow list who calls twice within 2 minutes gets through
• Calls are never rejected, only silenced, so you'll always see who called

**Follows your calendar**
• Quiet during every weekly standup, hidden during focus blocks
• Works with any calendar on your phone. No account login needed

**One tap away**
• Home-screen widget and Quick Settings tile to switch between My rules, Allow all and
  System DND
• Quiet for an hour, or let everything through for 30 minutes

**Nothing gets lost**
• Hidden notifications come back on their own when their time comes
• A log of what was quieted, with one tap to open the app

**Private by design**
• No internet permission: UnderTone cannot send your data anywhere
• No ads, no analytics, no accounts
• Everything stays encrypted on your phone
• Open source: github.com/yiannosparanomos/undertone
