# UnderTone privacy policy

*Effective date: to be set on first release. Last updated: 12 October 2026.*

UnderTone is an Android app that manages which notifications and calls reach you. It is
developed by Yiannos Paranomos ("we"). This policy explains what UnderTone accesses, why, and
what happens to it.

## The short version

- UnderTone has **no internet permission**. It cannot send anything off your phone.
- We **collect no data**. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.
- What UnderTone stores stays **on your phone, encrypted**, and is deleted automatically.

## What UnderTone accesses and why

| What | Why | Stored? |
|---|---|---|
| Notifications from your apps (app name, title, text, time) | To decide whether each one rings, stays silent, is held or is blocked | Only for silenced and held notifications, in the on-device log, for up to 7 days (adjustable) |
| Incoming call numbers | To apply your ring tier and the repeat-caller rule | The number and time of recent calls, on the device, for up to 7 days |
| Contacts (whether a number is saved or starred) | To know whether a caller is a contact or on your allow list | Only the extra people you add to an expanded allow list |
| Calendar events (title, time, calendar) | Only if you turn on calendar rules, to quiet the phone during matching events | No. Events are read when needed and not copied |
| Installed apps (launchable apps only) | To let you choose rules per app | Only the package names of apps you configure |
| Do Not Disturb setting | To keep the phone quiet while your rules decide what rings | No |

## Where data is kept

Everything UnderTone stores lives in an encrypted database inside the app's private storage on
your phone. It is excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup. Uninstalling UnderTone
deletes it all.

If you use **Export settings**, UnderTone writes a file with your rules (and the numbers on an
expanded allow list) to a location you choose. That file is under your control.

The optional **diagnostic log** records app package names and rule decisions, never
notification content, numbers or names. It leaves the phone only if you export and share it
yourself.

## Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any data. UnderTone contains no analytics, advertising or
crash-reporting libraries. If you install UnderTone from Google Play, Google may provide us
with anonymous crash and performance statistics through the Play Console, under Google's own
privacy policy.

## Children

UnderTone is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

We will update this page if UnderTone's data use changes, and note the change in the app's
release notes.
