# 1. Record architecture decisions

Date: 2026-10-12. Status: accepted.

## Context

UnderTone is built by one maintainer with Claude. Decisions made in conversation are easily
lost, and future sessions (human or AI) need to know why things are the way they are.

## Decision

Record every decision that is hard to reverse as a short Architecture Decision Record in
`docs/adr/`, numbered sequentially: context, decision, consequences. Superseded ADRs are kept
and marked as such.

## Consequences

Before changing something an ADR covers, read the ADR and either follow it or write a new one
that supersedes it.
