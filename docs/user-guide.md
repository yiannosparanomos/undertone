# UnderTone user guide

UnderTone decides what rings, what stays quiet and what waits until later. This guide covers
everyday use. Setup happens once, in the tour that opens on first launch.

## The three profiles

Switch from the home-screen widget, the Quick Settings tile, or the top of the Home screen.

- **My rules**: your setup is in charge. This is the normal mode.
- **Allow all**: UnderTone steps aside and your phone behaves normally. Anything held comes
  back. Use it when you're waiting for something important.
- **System DND**: Android's own Do Not Disturb takes over, with whatever you set in phone
  Settings.

## What each level does

| Level | You hear it | You see it when you unlock |
|---|---|---|
| Ring | Yes | Yes |
| Silent | No | Yes |
| Hidden | No | Not yet: it's in the held inbox and comes back by itself |
| Blocked | No | No, it's treated as spam |

Every app has a level **inside its hours** and one **outside its hours**. Teams, for example,
can ring on weekdays from 09:00 to 18:00 and be Hidden the rest of the time, so on Monday
morning you find the weekend's messages waiting.

## Setting up apps

1. **All apps**: pick one level for everything. Ring is a good start, so nothing goes missing.
2. **Focus apps**: give the apps that matter (Phone, Messenger, Mail, Teams…) their own level,
   hours and sound.
3. **As you go**: when an app annoys you, make it Silent or Blocked. UnderTone also suggests
   this when you keep swiping an app away without opening it.

## Blocking spam

Long-press an entry in the log or the Recent list and choose **Block app**, or **Block this
type** to block only that kind of notification (a shop's promotions but not its order
updates). Blocked apps are listed on the Apps screen, where you can unblock them.

## Held notifications

Hidden notifications wait in the **held inbox** (Home → Held). They come back on their own
when the app's hours start, all together with one quiet summary. You can also tap **Release
all**, or open any one of them from the inbox.

## Quick overrides

From the widget or Home: **Quiet for 1 hour**, **Quiet until…**, **Quiet until this event
ends**, or **Let everything through for 30 minutes**. Overrides end by themselves. Blocked
apps stay blocked even when you let everything through.

## Calendar rules

Rules → Calendar. Pick which calendars UnderTone may read (any calendar on your phone works).
A rule can target a recurring event (every *Weekly standup*), one event, a whole calendar, or
any event whose title contains a word. It says which apps may ring, what the others do, and
who may call. All-day events are ignored unless you turn them on.

When a calendar rule and an app's hours disagree, the stricter one wins.

## Calls

Calls → **Ring tier**: Everyone, Contacts, Allow list or Nobody. A call that isn't allowed
still comes through silently and shows as a missed call. UnderTone never rejects calls.

**Allow list**: your starred contacts, or an expanded list with extra people you add in
UnderTone.

**Repeat callers**: if someone calls again quickly, it may be urgent. Choose who can break
through (allow list or everyone), how many calls it takes (default 2) and how soon (2, 5 or
15 minutes, default 2).

## Sounds

While My rules is on, UnderTone plays the sounds for Ring apps itself. Sounds → pick one
default sound and, optionally, a different one per app; turn vibration on or off; set
UnderTone's own volume; tap **Test** to hear it.

## If something seems off

Open **Health check**. It shows whether every permission is still granted and whether your
phone's battery settings might stop UnderTone. If UnderTone is ever stopped, it switches Do Not
Disturb off and tells you, so your phone doesn't stay silent without you knowing.

## Privacy

UnderTone has no internet access. What it stores stays encrypted on your phone and the log
clears itself after 7 days. See the [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md).
